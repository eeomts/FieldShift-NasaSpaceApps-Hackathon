## AI (Super Intelligence) disclosure

This video was produced with AI assistance and carries a visible **"AI-generated (SI)"** watermark on every frame.

| Part of the video | How it was made |
|---|---|
| Video, illustrations, animation and captions | Generated with **Claude Opus 5.5** (Anthropic), as code that renders the animation frame by frame |
| Narrator character | Drawn by the AI from a photo of team member Guilherme Melo; his real photo is also shown |
| Narrator voice | Synthesized with **Chatterbox TTS** (Resemble AI), cloned from Guilherme Melo's own voice, with his consent |
| Voice of "Dona Marta" (a fictional farmer) | Synthetic: **Microsoft Ava** neural text-to-speech |
| Background music | Generated by code written with AI assistance; no copyrighted samples |
| Numbers on screen | Illustrative, marked "Illustrative example" in the video |

No AI-generated content in the video uses or modifies NASA logos, flags or mission identifiers, and nobody under 18 appears in it.

The video names MODIS, SMAP, GPM and NASA POWER. In this version of the app, the NASA data used in the analysis are **NASA POWER** and **SRTM**. SMAP, MODIS NDVI and GPM IMERG are on the [roadmap](05-limitations-roadmap.md). See [NASA Data](03-nasa-data.md).
